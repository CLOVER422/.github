# 🍀 CLOVER

### *A team of students. A passion for technology. A vision to build.*

Welcome to **Clover** — the GitHub organization of a team of technology enthusiasts from **Maharishi University of Information Technology (MUIT)**.

We are students who love exploring new technologies, turning ideas into reality, building innovative projects, and challenging ourselves through hackathons. From experimenting with new concepts to developing practical solutions, Clover is where our ideas come together and our projects take shape.

This organization serves as the central hub for **all projects built by Team Clover**.

We believe that the best way to learn technology is by building with it.

At Clover, we collaborate, experiment, solve problems, and continuously improve our skills through hands-on development. Whether we are working on a college project, exploring a new tech stack, or preparing for a hackathon, we aim to create something meaningful with every project.
